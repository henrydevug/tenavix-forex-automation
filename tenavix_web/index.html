FAQ

Does Tenavix guarantee profits?

No. The product focuses on disciplined automation and risk controls, not performance claims.

Is live trading enabled by default?

No. DEMO / SIMULATION is the default. Live trading requires explicit authorization after validation.

Where do risk controls run?

Primarily in the local MQL5 Expert Advisor so emergency protection is not dependent on backend availability.

How do I get a license key?

Register, log in, and add a trading account. The backend issues a license key for that MT5 account number.
